# Channel voice — first draft (edit me)

## Who we are

An English-language GTA gameplay channel on PS5, hosted by **Lucianus**: an animated avatar that reacts in real time instead of a face cam. The shorts are small stories of 30–60 seconds cut from long sessions (one or more parts edited together): chaos, fails, lucky escapes, funny reactions, small stories that land.

Lucianus's personality on the page: quick, deadpan, a bit unhinged. Black humor is the brand: dark, ironic captions about what happens in the game ("*parole: violated*", "now she can't 💀", "5 stars on Tripadvisor"). He laughs at himself first, and at the game's chaos and NPCs without mercy.

## How we sound

- American English, casual, spoken. Write like a friend texting a clip, not like a trailer.
- Short sentences. Present tense ("Lucianus steals a car", not "Lucianus stole a car").
- Specific beats generic: "a two-star chase for borrowing one car" beats "crazy police chase".
- Self-deprecating jokes and black humor about in-game situations are on brand. Keep it about the game: no jokes at real people or real-world groups, no drugs.
- Max one emoji per text, and only when it adds something. None in the hook.
- No ALL-CAPS words except an occasional single one for emphasis (in titles only).

## Never

- Lie about the clip. No "GTA 6 LEAK", "SECRET", "YOU WON'T BELIEVE" unless it is literally true.
- Spoil the punchline in the hook or the first half of the title.
- Describe gore or real-world violence. Game violence is "chaos", "a fight", "a shootout", never graphic.
- Name or tag other creators unless they are in the clip and it is a collab.
- Ask for likes or follows in the hook or title.

## Hook (text on screen in the first 1.5 s)

- 2–6 words. It opens a question the clip answers.
- Concrete situation, not a verdict: "He only borrowed the car" works, "Funniest moment ever" does not.
- It must still make sense with the sound off.

## Platforms

**YouTube Shorts**
- Title: 40–70 characters, the hook idea plus the game name when it fits naturally ("GTA 6", "GTA V").
- Description: 1–2 lines that add context, then hashtags.
- Hashtags: 3–5, always `#GTA6` or the right game tag, plus `#shorts` and 1–3 specific ones.

**Instagram Reels**
- Title: the first line of the caption, up to 60 characters, works as a standalone hook.
- Description: 1–3 short sentences, ends with a light question that invites comments ("Would you have stopped?").
- Hashtags: 3–5, mixing broad (`#gta`, `#gaming`) and specific.

**TikTok**
- Title: up to 60 characters, the most casual of the three.
- Description: one line, can be a reaction or an inside joke.
- Hashtags: 3–5, `#gta6` or the right game, `#gaming`, plus trending-safe specifics. No `#fyp` spam.

## Scoring a short (1–10)

Be strict. 7+ means "publish as is".
- Hook: does the first second make you stay?
- Clarity: does it work for someone who has never seen the channel?
- Payoff: is there a laugh, a twist or a release?
- Loop: does the ending flow back into the start?
- Risk: anything that could get it limited (graphic violence at the start, copyrighted music, cutscene-only content) lowers the score.
